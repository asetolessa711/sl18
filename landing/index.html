Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get an invite token?

Invite tokens are distributed via email from beta@waliinstudio.com. Contact your referrer or email us to request access.

Why did my token expire?

Tokens expire after 72 hours for security. Request a new token from your contact or email beta@waliinstudio.com.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use TLS 1.2+ encryption, WAF protection, and the beta environment is completely isolated from production.

How do I report bugs?

Use the "Report Issue" button in the dashboard or email beta-bugs@waliinstudio.com with details.

What's the difference between Creator and Customer cohorts?

Creators are social media influencers who receive demo clips and brand assets to promote Waliin Studio. Customers are content consumers who get early access to preview and provide feedback on our content library.

How do I become a Creator?

Apply for Creator access by emailing creators@waliinstudio.com with your social media handles and follower count.
